ASCII Avatar Lab

Standalone motion prototype for the girl avatar. This page treats media/1.txt through media/13.txt as ordered keyframes on one timeline, fills the in-between frames in JavaScript, and drives the playhead by state plus audio energy instead of hard frame swaps. The interpolation now uses smaller local tiles so the face can bend and drift in pieces.
